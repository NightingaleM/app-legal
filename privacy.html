Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 4, 2026
We do not collect any personal data.

Overview

Crazy Brick Ball (“the App”) is a single-player casual game. We do not collect, transmit, or share any personal information from or about you. The App contains no advertising, analytics, or tracking components.

Information We Do Not Collect

  • We do not collect your name, email, phone number, location, or any contact data.
  • We do not collect device identifiers such as IDFA, advertising identifiers, or a unique device ID.
  • We do not transmit any game data to our servers — the App has no network back-end.

Information Stored Locally on Your Device

The following data is stored only on your device, using the device’s own local storage, and never leaves your device:

  • Game progress and save data (levels, scores, settings, unlocks).
  • An internal app identifier that the App generates locally to keep its own data consistent. It is random, stored only on your device, and is not shared with us or anyone else.

Because this data lives only on your device, you can erase it completely at any time by uninstalling the App.

Third-Party Services

None. This version of the App does not use any third-party advertising, analytics, crash-reporting, or tracking SDKs. If this changes in a future update, we will update this Privacy Policy and disclose the relevant services before any data is handled.

Children’s Privacy

The App is a casual game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Because the App collects no data at all, no special protections for children’s data are required.

Your Rights (GDPR / CCPA and similar laws)

Because we collect no personal data, there is no data about you for us to access, correct, export, or delete. You remain in full control: all App data is on your device and is removed when you uninstall the App.

Data Security

All data stays on your device under your control. No data is transmitted over the network, so there is no transmission-related risk to protect against.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the “Effective date” at the top of this page.
