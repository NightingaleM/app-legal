FAQ

Is the game free?
Yes. This launch version is completely free, with no ads and no in-app purchases — just download and play.
Does the game upload any of my data?
No. The game is fully offline. All your progress and settings stay on your device and are never transmitted anywhere. See our Privacy Policy.
How do I erase my progress?
Uninstalling the app removes all game data from your device.
Will you add ads or in-app purchases later?
Future updates may introduce optional, non-intrusive ads and purchases (for example, a one-time “remove ads” option). The core game will always stay free to enjoy.
The game feels slow / a ball got stuck / something looks wrong.
Please email us with a short description (and a screenshot if you can). Telling us your device model and iOS version helps a lot.
