Winnow: Swipe to Keep

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 8, 2026
We do not collect any personal data.

Overview

Winnow (“the App”) is a photo cleanup app that helps you review your library one photo at a time. We do not collect, transmit, or share any personal information from or about you. The App has no account system, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics, and no tracking components. It has no server of its own.

Information We Do Not Collect

  • We do not collect your name, email, phone number, location, or any contact data.
  • We do not collect device identifiers such as IDFA, advertising identifiers, or a unique device ID.
  • We do not upload your photos or any App data to any server — the App has no network back-end of its own.

Photos and Permissions

The App requests access to your photo library so it can display your photos and perform the cleanup you choose. It reads photo capture dates so it can start from your oldest photos. All photo processing happens on your device.

When you confirm deletions at the end of a cleanup session, photos are moved to the system “Recently Deleted” album, where they remain recoverable for 30 days. This is iOS’s own mechanism, managed entirely by the system; the App never permanently erases a photo on its own.

Optional Connection to Apple iCloud

By default, the App works fully offline.

If — and only if — you enable the “iCloud Originals Loading” option in Settings, the App connects solely to Apple iCloud, through the system Photos framework, to download your own photo originals to your own device for sharper display. This applies to your photos only.

Even with this option on, nothing is collected or uploaded by us: the only network peer the App ever talks to is Apple iCloud, and the only data transferred is a download of your own photos to your device. Apple iCloud is a service provided by Apple and governed by Apple’s own terms and privacy policy. You can turn this option off at any time in the App’s settings.

Information Stored Locally on Your Device

The following data is stored only on your device, using the device’s own local storage, and never leaves your device:

  • Cleanup session data — your in-progress queue, choices made during a session (kept / to-delete / later), and the pending box, so you can leave and resume.
  • Review markers — which photos you have already gone through in past sessions, so already-reviewed photos are not shown again.
  • Settings — gesture orientation, haptics, and the iCloud option described above.
  • A local founding-member marker, derived on-device from your original App Store purchase date via StoreKit’s local verification. It is never sent anywhere.

Because this data lives only on your device, you can erase it completely at any time by uninstalling the App.

Third-Party Services

None. This version of the App does not use any third-party advertising, analytics, crash-reporting, or tracking SDKs. The only external service that can ever be involved is Apple iCloud, as described above, under your explicit control. If this changes in a future update, we will update this Privacy Policy and disclose the relevant services before any data is handled.

Children’s Privacy

The App is a photo cleanup utility and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Because the App collects no data at all, no special protections for children’s data are required.

Your Rights (GDPR / CCPA and similar laws)

Because we collect no personal data, there is no data about you for us to access, correct, export, or delete. You remain in full control: all App data is on your device and is removed when you uninstall the App.

Data Security

All data stays on your device under your control. Apart from the optional Apple iCloud download described above, no data is transmitted over the network, so there is no transmission-related risk to protect against.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the “Effective date” at the top of this page.

概述

Winnow(以下简称"本应用")是一款照片清理工具,帮助你一张一张地整理相册。我们不收集、传输或共享您的任何个人信息。本应用没有账号系统、无需登录、不含广告、分析或追踪组件,也没有自己的服务器。

我们不收集的信息

  • 我们不收集您的姓名、邮箱、电话、位置或任何联系方式。
  • 我们不收集设备标识符,例如 IDFA、广告标识符或唯一设备 ID。
  • 我们不会将您的照片或任何应用数据上传到任何服务器 —— 本应用没有自己的网络后端。

照片与权限

本应用请求访问您的照片图库,用于展示照片并执行您选择的清理操作;它会读取照片的拍摄时间,以便从最老的照片开始整理。所有照片处理均在本机完成。

当您在清理结束时确认删除,照片会被移入系统的"最近删除"相簿,在那里保留 30 天可恢复。这是 iOS 自身的机制,完全由系统管理;本应用绝不会自行永久抹除任何照片。

可选的 Apple iCloud 连接

默认情况下,本应用完全离线运行。

当且仅当您在设置中开启"iCloud 原图加载"后,本应用才会仅连接 Apple iCloud(通过系统照片框架),将您自己的照片原图下载到您自己的设备上,以获得更清晰的显示。这只涉及您自己的照片。

即使开启该选项,我们也不会收集或上传任何数据:本应用唯一可能连接的网络对象是 Apple iCloud,唯一发生的数据传输是把您自己的照片下载到您的设备。Apple iCloud 是 Apple 提供的服务,受 Apple 自身的条款与隐私政策约束。您可以随时在本应用设置中关闭该选项。

仅存储在您设备本地的信息

以下数据仅存储在您的设备上(使用设备自身的本地存储),从不离开您的设备:

  • 清理会话数据 —— 进行中的队列、会话内的决策(保留/待删/待处理)与待处理盒子,便于您中途退出后继续。
  • 已整理标记 —— 哪些照片在之前的会话中已经过目,使已整理的照片不再重复出现。
  • 设置 —— 手势方向、触感反馈,以及上文所述的 iCloud 选项。
  • 本地的创始用户标记 —— 通过 StoreKit 的本地校验,在设备上根据您最初的 App Store 购买日期生成,从不发送到任何地方。

由于这些数据只存在于您的设备上,您随时可以通过卸载本应用将其完全清除。
