Frequently asked questions

Where do deleted photos go?

Photos you confirm for deletion are moved to the system “Recently Deleted” album and stay recoverable for 30 days. Winnow never permanently erases a photo on its own — the final step is always a review list you control.

How do I start a cleanup?

Four ways: from your oldest photos (the default), the last 100 photos, a random 100, or a custom selection where you hand-pick photos by month.

I’m left-handed. Can I swap the gestures?

Yes. In Settings you can swap the keep direction between right and left; the delete (up) and later (down) gestures stay fixed.

Why do some iCloud photos look blurry?

If you use iCloud Photos with “Optimize Storage”, only low-resolution copies may be on the device. You can enable “iCloud Originals Loading” in Settings to load your own originals from Apple iCloud for sharper display. Wi-Fi is recommended. Even with this on, nothing is collected or uploaded — see our Privacy Policy.

Does Winnow upload my photos?

No. All processing happens on your device. There is no account, no sign-in, and no server. See the Privacy Policy for details.

删除的照片去哪了?

您确认删除的照片会被移入系统"最近删除"相簿,30 天内可恢复。Winnow 绝不会自行永久抹除照片 —— 最后一步永远是一份由您逐项复核的确认清单。

怎么开始一次清理?

四种方式:从最老的照片开始(默认)、清理最近 100 张、随机 100 张,或在自定义选择中按月份手动挑选。

我是左撇子,手势能换吗?

可以。在设置中可把"保留"方向在左/右之间互换;"待删"(上滑)与"待处理"(下滑)固定不变。

为什么有些 iCloud 照片看着模糊?

如果您开启了 iCloud 照片的"优化储存空间",设备上可能只存有低分辨率版本。可在设置中开启"iCloud 原图加载",从 Apple iCloud 加载您自己的原图以获得更清晰的显示,建议在 Wi-Fi 下进行。即使开启,也不会收集或上传任何数据 —— 详见隐私政策。

Winnow 会上传我的照片吗?

不会。所有处理都在您的设备上完成。没有账号、无需登录、没有服务器。详见隐私政策。
